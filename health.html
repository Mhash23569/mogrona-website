Sweet Swaps

One sachet, two teaspoons

Every Mogrona sachet is as sweet as two teaspoons of sugar, with zero calories. Swap it in wherever you would normally reach for the sugar bowl: chai, coffee, cold drinks, yogurt and oats.

If you usually take one teaspoon of sugar, start with half a sachet and adjust to your taste.

Weight management

Sugar adds calories quickly: one level teaspoon contains about 16. Mogrona has zero calories, so replacing the sugar in your daily drinks is an easy way to cut calories without giving up the taste you enjoy.

Diabetes

Mogrona contains no sugar, and the pack is labelled as suitable for weight control and diabetes. Managing diabetes is personal, so please follow the advice of your doctor or dietitian about what fits your plan.

What is inside

Just two ingredients: monk fruit extract and erythritol. Mogrona contains no stevia, and it is plant based, gluten free and halal.
